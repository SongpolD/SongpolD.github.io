01 · Situation · Jan–Aug 2025

Baseline Setup

  • Overall ROAS: 19.08
  • Sponsored Max Store — Average daily budget
  • Sponsored Discovery — Average daily budget
  • Sponsored MSA — Total budget

Key Insight

  • Top products: high revenue, low clicks → under-budgeted
  • High-click product: clicks high, revenue low → inefficient spend
  • Fix: split campaigns by product performance tier
02 · Action · Sep–Nov 2025

Budget Restructure

  • Sponsored Max Store: 50%
  • Sponsored MSA: 50%
  • Removed Sponsored Discovery

Optimization

  • Max Store → Average daily + D-Day surge
  • Closely optimize D-Day, D-Day−1, D-Day+1
  • MSA → Total budget, campaign-based
